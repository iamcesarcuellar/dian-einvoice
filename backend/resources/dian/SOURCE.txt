The schemas in xsd/ come from the DIAN e-invoicing toolbox v1.9 (Caja de herramientas FE - V1.9 - (v2026-2)), https://www.dian.gov.co/impuestos/factura-electronica/Documents/Caja-de-herramientas-FE_V19_v2026.zip, downloaded on 2026-11-17 (zip SHA-256 868bc7ac184f646b47a61e2b3a978c0c195fc958e8ec7b54e95010717aa477e5). They are unchanged: XSD/common, and XSD/maindoc/UBL-Invoice-2.1.xsd and DIAN_UBL_Structures.xsd since 2026-11-17; UBL-AttachedDocument-2.1.xsd, UBL-CreditNote-2.1.xsd and UBL-DebitNote-2.1.xsd since 2026-11-18. make dian-toolbox downloads the toolbox again, checks its SHA-256 and compares these schemas with its own, byte for byte. NOTICE gives their owners and terms, and scripts/security/third-party-files.json their SHA-256.

This folder is the only copy of the schemas: the simulator image copies it when it is built (build context dian-xsd in compose.yaml).

The rest of the toolbox is not redistributed. Its examples, schematron and code lists stay in backend/storage/app/dian-toolbox, which git ignores, and only make dian-schematron reads them. The example invoice that was kept in examples/ ("Ejemplificación Impuesto especial para la estabilidad fiscal.xml"), which nothing read, was removed on 2026-11-22. The genericode lists ConceptoNotaCredito-2.1.gc and ConceptoNotaDebito-2.1.gc were removed on 2026-11-19: the credit note list is older than table 13.2.4 of the annex (it has no code 6, and its 5 is "Otros"), so the concepts and their official names come from the annex's tables, in the enums CreditNoteConcept and DebitNoteConcept.
