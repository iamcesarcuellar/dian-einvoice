# Documentation

Every document of the repository, one line each. The [README](../README.md) is the way in: what the project is, how to run it with `make demo`, and a five-minute tour.

## The project

- [README.md](../README.md): what dian-einvoice is and what it shows, the quick start, the tour, the architecture at a glance, its limits and its roadmap.
- [architecture.md](architecture.md): the application's architecture: its context, its modules and how they talk, the data model, a document's life cycle, the design decisions and the guarantees the tests hold.
- [api-guide.md](api-guide.md): a practical guide to the public API: first steps with curl, idempotency, errors, pagination, rate limits, versions and signed webhooks, every example run by `make docs-examples`.
- [dian-conformance.md](dian-conformance.md): what the project implements of the DIAN's technical annex 1.9, how to check each point, what is not implemented, and the official sources.
- [cost.md](cost.md): what the prod stage would cost a month in us-east-1, an estimate generated by `make cost` from the prices and the assumptions of `scripts/cost`.
- [limitations.md](limitations.md): what the project does not do, or does only in part: the DIAN, the signature, the XML, the delivery, the API, the emulated cloud, the edge, observability and the tests.
- [threat-model.md](threat-model.md): the assets, the actors, the trust boundaries, the threats with their mitigations, and the risks that remain.
- [third-party-licenses.md](third-party-licenses.md): every production dependency with its version and license, and the files of others in the repository, generated by `make licenses` and checked by `make security`.
- [NOTICE](../NOTICE): the files of others in the repository, such as the official schemas and the fonts, with their notices and terms; the MIT License does not cover them.
- [SECURITY.md](../SECURITY.md): how to report a vulnerability privately, and what is in scope.
- [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md): the repository's rules and the checks a change runs.
- [LICENSE](../LICENSE): the project's MIT License.

## The cloud, as code and on an emulator

- [infrastructure.md](infrastructure.md): the production architecture on AWS, the CDK app and the serverless service, the prod stage's network and database, the edge, and what serves each piece locally and in AWS.
- [observability.md](observability.md): the log line and its schema, the correlation id, the metrics, the alarms and their objectives, the notifier, and how to follow an invoice.
- [deployment.md](deployment.md): the one deployment script for every stage, what a real account needs, the first deployment, going back and tearing down; never run against an account.
